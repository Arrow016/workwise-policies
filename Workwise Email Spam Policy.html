Anti-Spam and Acceptable Email Policy

WorkWise Robotics is committed to responsible email and digital communication practices. No one may advertise our domains, products or services through spam.

Last updated October 25, 2026Applies to employees, contractors and everyone acting on our behalf

Our commitment

Our corporate domains, websites, trademarks, products, and services may not be advertised or promoted through spam or unsolicited bulk email.

Who this applies to

This prohibition applies to WorkWise Robotics employees, contractors, affiliates, agencies, vendors, service providers, partners, and any other third party acting on our behalf.

  • Employees
  • Contractors
  • Affiliates
  • Agencies
  • Vendors
  • Service providers
  • Partners
  • Any third party acting for us

Prohibited activities

The following activities are prohibited:

  • Sending unsolicited bulk email that advertises or links to a WorkWise Robotics website or domain.

  • Using purchased, harvested, scraped, or otherwise improperly obtained email lists for bulk advertising of our website.

  • Continuing to send promotional messages to recipients who have opted out or requested that communication stop.

  • Using misleading sender information, deceptive subject lines, unauthorized identities, or other methods intended to obscure the source or commercial nature of a message.

  • Allowing an affiliate, contractor, vendor, or other third party to advertise a WorkWise Robotics domain through prohibited unsolicited messaging.

Enforcement

WorkWise Robotics may suspend or terminate relationships with employees, contractors, vendors, affiliates, or service providers that violate this policy.

Reporting abuse

Reports of suspected abuse involving a WorkWise Robotics domain are investigated promptly. Where appropriate, recipients are suppressed from further communications and the source of the activity is reviewed to prevent recurrence.

Questions or abuse reports regarding this policy may be directed to info@workwiserobotics.com.
